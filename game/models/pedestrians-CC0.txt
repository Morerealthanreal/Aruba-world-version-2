ARUBA WORLD PEDESTRIAN CHARACTER ASSETS
=======================================

Source characters and animations
--------------------------------

Maya and Sam locomotion collections from the Innerscene 3D Parts Library:

https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/locomotion-collection-maya-3d-person-animated-1499b2fd
https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/locomotion-collection-sam-3d-person-animated-dc23800f

The source pages identify these as original Innerscene characters and motion
adapted from MakeHuman/MPFB CC0 body, rig, and skin assets. Both source files
are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:

https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/terms

Project modifications
---------------------

The ped_maya_*, ped_maya_dress_*, and ped_sam_* files are modified derivatives:

- The LOD files retain Idle_Standing and Walk_Relaxed as a fallback.
  The live walk, run, and idle cycles are the separate locomotion.glb clips.
- Add opaque layered hair cards and, for the dress variant, an opaque rigged
  flowing skirt.
- Bake skin, face, hair, and fabric into a hand-painted 4x4 texture atlas.
- Add near and reduced mid-distance mesh LODs.
- Compress textures as KTX2/ETC1S and geometry/animation with Meshopt.

The near-LOD face paintings in models/faces/ (face-maya.jpg, face-dress.jpg,
face-sam.jpg) are project-made oil-style portraits. They appear on a curved
card parented to the head of the two or three closest people, within about
8 m. Each card is 126 triangles, so a crowd adds at most a few hundred
triangles. They are not part of the Innerscene or MakeHuman sources.

The character meshes are not Quaternius bodies. The walk, run, and idle
cycles in locomotion.glb are retargeted from the Quaternius Universal
Animation Library (CC0). See CREDITS.md.
