Basis Universal KTX2 transcoder
===============================

basis_transcoder.js and basis_transcoder.wasm are the unmodified prebuilt
Basis Universal web transcoder distributed with Three.js r186.

Upstream: https://github.com/BinomialLLC/basis_universal
License: Apache License 2.0 (see LICENSE.txt)
Attribution: see NOTICE.txt

These files are loaded by Three.js KTX2Loader to transcode the pedestrian
texture atlases into a GPU-supported compressed texture format at runtime.
