The yellow shirt, blue jeans, cap, sunglasses, and sandals follow the
Oranjestad street photos listed in `refs/people/aruba-outfits.md`. The
photos are not pasted on. The oil shading is a shader on this mesh: smoothed
normals, brush strokes, warmer skin, almond eyes, hair strands, cloth folds,
and a soft golden rim. No new textures.
