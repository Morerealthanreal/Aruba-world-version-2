# Aruba outfit notes

These photos are clothing references only. They are not textures, and they are not pasted onto the people. Each person below is a CC0 Quaternius mesh, recolored and given hats, sunglasses, bags, or sandals to follow what the photo actually shows. Everyone stays fully clothed. Nothing is sheer.

Packs, both CC0:

- Women: [Ultimate Modular Women](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularwomen.html), files from the public pack, stored as `game/models/people/w-*.glb`
- Men: [Ultimate Modular Men](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularcharacters.html), stored as `game/models/people/m-*.glb`

Each file keeps that character's own skeleton and the pack's Idle, Walk, and Run.

## Photos

1. [Bonbini Festival Aruba](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bonbini_Festival_Aruba.jpeg), DutchCaribbean, CC BY-SA 4.0. A woman in a white knee-length dress, straw hat, dark sunglasses, sandals, and a small bag. A man in a light blue short-sleeve shirt, pale shorts, a dark cap, and sandals.
2. [Oranjestad Downtown](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Oranjestad_Downtown_(3558373633).jpg), Roger W, CC BY 2.0. A bright yellow T-shirt with blue jeans, a baseball cap, and sandals. Another person in a blue graphic T-shirt and jeans.
3. [Oranjestad Downtown](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Oranjestad_Downtown_(3559199684).jpg), Roger W, CC BY 2.0. Coral and light shirts, khaki shorts, caps, sunglasses, and sandals on the shopping street.
4. [Aruba Flea Market](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Aruba_Flea_Market.jpg), David Burley, CC0. A white sundress, wide straw hat, sunglasses, sandals, and a light tote at the Oranjestad wharf market.
5. [Palm Beach, Aruba](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Palm_Beach,_Aruba_(4901990402).jpg), Matt Kieffer, CC BY 2.0. Beach visitors in light shirts, shorts, and hats along the sand.
6. [Activemirror at Casibari](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Activemirror_live_at_Casibari_Rock_Formation_04.jpg), CC BY 4.0. Everyday black graphic T-shirt, blue jeans, and a baseball cap.

The packs do not include a separate elderly body. Older people are the same adult meshes, shorter, with grey hair and a hat. Trousers stay long where the mesh has no leg underneath to turn into shorts. Shorts are used only on the meshes that already have them.
