# Walk-in interiors

Famous places the player can enter on foot. Each interior is built from meshes and materials (no photos pasted into the game), loads only when the player is near, and keeps the existing exterior, sign and roof. A doorway is cut in the hero wall and in pedestrian collision; counters and tables still block.

## Done

- **The Pastechi House** (Oranjestad, Caya G.F. Betico Croes 42). Open front under the green PASTECHI HOUSE sign, wood counter with a pastry warmer, menu board (cheese, ham & cheese, beef, chicken, tuna, keri keri, lobster, croquette and the other house snacks), small red chairs and tables, cream interior with the green band and red base from the street facade.
- **McDonald's Palm Beach** (J.E. Irausquin Boulevard). Storefront opens on the clear stretch beside the sign. The yellow arch blade and the roadside sign stay. Grey tile dining room, wood order counter with a red fascia and yellow pickup strip, menu boards, two order kiosks, red chairs and grey tables.
- **Starbucks Palm Beach** (Paseo Herencia, J.E. Irausquin Boulevard 382A). Entrance under the siren sign on the south face. Wood bar with a green front, pastry case, drink menu, cafe tables and dark green chairs. The sign, green band and roof stay.
- **Palm Beach Plaza** (east rotunda, J.E. Irausquin Boulevard). The blue glass under the PALM BEACH PLAZA canopy opens. Inside: a tiled hall, Skechers and Niel's shopfronts, a Caribbean Cinemas counter, benches, and a hanging directory. The canopy, rotunda, and shop signs on the outside stay.
- **Hard Rock Cafe Palm Beach** (South Beach Centre). The north wall opens under the yellow HARD ROCK CAFE sign. Inside: a dark dining room, wood bar with a red front, stools, menu, tables with red chairs, and a guitar on the back wall. The sign, gold roof trim, and the roof guitar stay.
- **Radisson Blu Aruba** (Palm Beach). The north podium opens through the blue glass bays. Inside: a cream and pale-blue lobby, a navy reception desk, a Radisson Blu board, The Clover coffee counter, and blue chairs. The tower, balconies, and the Radisson Blu sign stay.
- **Hyatt Regency Aruba** (Palm Beach, J.E. Irausquin Boulevard). The ground floor under the HYATT REGENCY sign opens. Inside: a cream lobby, a terracotta band, a wood reception desk, and a board for reception, the casino, and the beach. The sign, balconies, and roofs stay.
